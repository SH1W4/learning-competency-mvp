# Go-To-Market Working Model

> **Status:** hypothesis. This document defines questions and an initial operating model; it does not assert validated demand, pricing or traction.

## Buying and usage roles

The team should distinguish:

- **Economic buyer:** who can authorize a pilot or purchase;
- **Problem owner:** who experiences the competency-development/measurement problem;
- **Program owner:** who structures the trail;
- **Learner:** who performs activities and produces evidence;
- **Reviewer:** who evaluates evidence and state transitions;
- **Verifier:** who needs to inspect the resulting proof.

These roles may be the same person in an early pilot.

## Initial wedge

The first market wedge should be a narrow organizational competency-development workflow where:

- the competency matters to an identifiable program;
- learning activities already exist or can be created quickly;
- evidence is naturally produced;
- review is feasible;
- the value of a structured state can be demonstrated.

The exact wedge remains to be validated.

## Pilot model

A potential pilot sequence:

~~~text
DISCOVERY
  ↓
WORKFLOW MAPPING
  ↓
SMALL PILOT
  ↓
MEASURE EVIDENCE / REVIEW FRICTION
  ↓
VALIDATE VALUE
  ↓
EXPAND WITHIN PROGRAM
~~~

## Evidence of demand

Strong signals:

- request for a pilot;
- permission to observe or map a real workflow;
- access to relevant anonymized data;
- introduction to the budget/problem owner;
- concrete implementation commitment;
- willingness to pay or formal procurement exploration.

Weak signals:

- compliments;
- generic interest;
- “cool idea”;
- social engagement without commitment.

Do not report strong signals unless they actually occur.

## Pricing

**Not defined.**

Possible future models may include program subscription, organizational SaaS, implementation/service fees or institutional contracts. These are hypotheses only.

## Expansion hypothesis

If the core loop is validated, expansion may occur through additional competencies, programs, organizations and verification use cases.

Do not expand the MVP until the core loop is proven.

## Positioning principle

Lead with:

**competency development + evidence + review + verifiable state**

rather than leading with:

**blockchain + certificates + AI**.

This keeps the product thesis independent of any single infrastructure choice.
