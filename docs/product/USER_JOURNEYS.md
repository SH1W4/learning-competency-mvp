# User Journeys

> **Status:** working product specification. This document operationalizes the current thesis; it does not establish validated market facts.

## Purpose

Describe the minimum journeys through which the product connects organizational need, competency development, evidence, review, state and verification.

## Primary journey

~~~text
ORGANIZATION
  ↓
Competency desired
  ↓
Short trail
  ↓
PERSON
  ↓
Activities
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
AI interpretation
  ↓
Human review
  ↓
Competency state
  ↓
Attestation
  ↓
Verification
~~~

## Organization

1. Identify a competency need.
2. Define the desired competency.
3. Configure a short development trail.
4. Observe evidence produced during the trail.
5. Review the interpreted evidence.
6. Register a resulting competency state when the defined criteria are met.
7. Issue an attestation when the MVP flow supports it.
8. Allow an authorized verifier to inspect the proof.

## Person

1. Enters a defined trail.
2. Performs activities.
3. Produces evidence linked to those activities.
4. Receives structured interpretation of the evidence.
5. May receive human review/feedback.
6. Progresses through defined competency states.
7. Can use the resulting attestation as a verifiable representation of the recorded state.

## Reviewer

1. Receives evidence and AI interpretation.
2. Distinguishes source material from AI inference.
3. Accepts, corrects, rejects or requests additional evidence.
4. Records the review context.
5. Enables or prevents the corresponding state transition according to the defined rules.

## Verifier

1. Receives an attestation reference.
2. Resolves the subject, competency and state represented.
3. Checks the attestation and relevant integrity data.
4. Uses only the information the verification mechanism actually supports.

## MVP boundary

The journey must remain demonstrable with one competency, one short trail, one person, limited evidence formats, assisted AI interpretation, explicit human review, a persisted state, an attestation and a verification path.

This document does not define recruiting, marketplace, LMS replacement, universal credentialing or broad institutional integrations.
