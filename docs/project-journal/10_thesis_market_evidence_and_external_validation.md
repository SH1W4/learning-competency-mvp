# 10 — Thesis, Market Evidence & External Validation

**Date:** October 2026  
**Phase:** M4

## Context

The project needed to separate three different questions:

- Is the underlying organizational problem real?
- Is LASTRO's proposed mechanism technically credible?
- Has commercial demand for LASTRO been validated?

These questions cannot be answered by the same evidence.

## What changed

A thesis evidence matrix was added in `research/04_MARKET_VALIDATION_EVIDENCE.md`, connecting the product thesis to external sources including WEF, W3C, ACL, and UNESCO.

Recent market evidence was also incorporated into the public product narrative, including evidence concerning skills gaps, rapidly changing AI-exposed skills, and workforce capability barriers.

## Decision

External research is evidence for **problem context and thesis support**, not proof of product-market fit.

The project therefore avoids converting:

**industry pain → automatic commercial validation**

The same discipline applies to technical claims: standards, research, and external literature can support plausibility or context without proving that LASTRO has already achieved those outcomes.

## Result

The narrative became more evidence-grounded while preserving claim discipline.

The current position is:

> The problem is externally supported. The proposed architecture is implemented and demonstrated at MVP scope. Commercial demand, willingness to pay, ROI, and market superiority remain hypotheses to validate.

This distinction is now part of the project's evaluation posture.
