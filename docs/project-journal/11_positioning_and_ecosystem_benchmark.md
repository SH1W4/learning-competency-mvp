# 11 — Positioning & Ecosystem Benchmark

**Date:** October 2026  
**Phase:** M4

## Context

As the technical MVP stabilized, the project needed a clearer answer to a strategic question:

**Where does LASTRO fit relative to existing credential, skills, talent, learning, and attestation systems?**

The positioning therefore moved away from generic “learning competency” language and toward the narrower capability-verification thesis.

## Product positioning

The public narrative was consolidated around:

> **LASTRO — Capability Evidence & Verification Infrastructure**

The central product distinction became:

**Learning is not the same as demonstrated capability.**

LASTRO is not positioned as an LMS, generic credential platform, or blockchain credential product. Its proposed role is an evidence and verification layer connecting observable work to a bounded competency state.

## Ecosystem benchmark

A dedicated benchmark was added in `research/05_COLOSSEUM_ECOSYSTEM_BENCHMARK.md`.

Public descriptions of adjacent Colosseum projects were reviewed across evidence, interpretation, independent verification, consensus, competency state, attestation, and public verification.

The benchmark included projects such as LearnProof, VeriCred, Solana Matcher, SpineDAO, Strikesense, and Rei.

## Decision

The benchmark may support a **research-backed differentiation hypothesis**, but it must not be presented as proof of absolute market uniqueness.

The absence of an identified equivalent in public material is not proof that no equivalent exists.

## Result

LASTRO's differentiation is now expressed as a specific workflow hypothesis:

**WORK → EVIDENCE → INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION → CONSENSUS → COMPETENCY STATE → ATTESTATION → PUBLIC VERIFICATION**

This is stronger and more defensible than claiming generic novelty around credentials, AI, skills, or blockchain.
