## Research boundary

The research published here provides context, prior art, and bounded interpretations. It is not a substitute for the current product contract, implementation, tests, or reproducible proof.

Research does not by itself establish product-market fit, willingness to pay, product superiority, academic novelty, patentability, universal competency assessment, or universal conformance.

A public claim must be traceable to implementation, tests, a reproducible artifact, or a cited external source. Unresolved hypotheses must remain explicitly qualified.
