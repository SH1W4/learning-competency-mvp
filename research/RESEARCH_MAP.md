# Research Map — Public Scope

This map covers the research currently published to support LASTRO's bounded MVP claims. It is not a complete archive of every project hypothesis or internal planning artifact.

## Public research path

1. [Related work and external evidence](./RELATED_WORK_AND_EVIDENCE.md)

## Questions addressed

- What can observable work and structured evidence support about a bounded competency claim?
- How do evidence integrity, interpretation, verification, consensus, resolution, state, and attestation differ?
- What do existing standards and adjacent systems establish, and what remains outside their scope?
- Which claims are supported by external sources, implementation, tests, or reproducible proof?

## Evidence boundaries

Research can establish context, identify related work, and motivate design choices. It does not by itself establish that LASTRO is novel, commercially validated, superior to all alternatives, or universally applicable.

The current product contract and executable implementation define the MVP. Public claims must remain traceable to the current source, tests, reproducible artifacts, or cited external evidence.

## Promotion rule

A research conclusion should affect product behavior only when the relevant decision is explicit in the product/architecture contract and reflected in implementation and tests. Hypotheses remain hypotheses until supported by appropriate evidence.
