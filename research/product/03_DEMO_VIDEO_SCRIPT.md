# LASTRO MVP: 3-Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard

**Objective:** Pitch the problem, introduce the LASTRO M4 Consensus Engine, showcase the UI, and prove the Solana Devnet anchoring.
**Target Length:** 3 Minutes
**Tone:** Confident, highly technical but accessible, visionary.

---

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Problem (The Hook)
**Visual:** 
- Fast-paced montage of generic online course certificates, AI-generated essays, and a confused recruiter looking at a resume.
- **Text on screen:** "Degrees are losing value. AI can fake any essay. How do we prove actual human capability?"

**Voiceover:**
*"The internet is flooded with digital certificates, but they share a fatal flaw: they lack verifiable proof of capability. As AI makes it trivial to fake assignments, traditional education credentials are losing their trust. We need a way to transition from blind trust to cryptographic proof. Enter LASTRO."*

## 0:30 - 1:15 | The Solution (The Engine)
**Visual:** 
- High-quality architectural diagram showing the flow: `Evidence -> Integrity Checks -> AI Interpretation -> Consensus -> Solana Blockchain`.
- Zoom in on the "Consensus Engine" node, highlighting the "Fail-Closed" and "Human Adjudication" routes.

**Voiceover:**
*"LASTRO is not just another learning platform; it’s an infrastructure for Verifiable Capabilities. 
When a learner submits evidence, we don't just ask an AI to grade it. Our proprietary M4 Consensus Engine runs deterministic integrity checks alongside AI interpretation. Only when all mechanisms reach a strict agreement does the system transition the state. If there's a conflict, the system blocks automatic advancement and flags it for human adjudication. We eliminate AI hallucination from the credentialing process."*

## 1:15 - 2:15 | Platform Demo (The UI & UX)
**Visual:** 
- Screen recording of the LASTRO Next.js application (with JP's premium UI).
- The user navigates to the "M4 Canonical Runtime" screen.
- The cursor hovers over the "Consensus Result" card showing "AGREEMENT".
- Scrolls down to show the "Evidence Integrity" and "AI Interpretation" signals.

**Voiceover:**
*"Let’s look at the Canonical Runtime Projection. This interface acts as a transparent, read-only window into the learner's journey. Here, we see a competency record that has reached the 'Demonstrated' state. 
You can inspect the exact evidence submitted, see the deterministic integrity checks, and review the AI's confidence score. Everything is completely transparent and immutable."*

## 2:15 - 2:45 | Web3 Proof (The Solana Explorer)
**Visual:** 
- Cursor clicks on the "Open transaction in Solana Explorer" link in the UI.
- Transition to the Solana Explorer page, highlighting the successful transaction and the `Record Hash`.

**Voiceover:**
*"But we don't expect you to just trust our servers. Once the consensus engine approves a competency, the final state and its cryptographic hash are anchored on the Solana blockchain. 
This provides the learner with an immutable, censorship-resistant Verifiable Claim. The proof belongs to the learner forever, independently verifiable by any employer, without relying on our platform's uptime."*

## 2:45 - 3:00 | Conclusion & Vision (The Outro)
**Visual:** 
- The LASTRO logo on a dark, premium background.
- **Text on screen:** "LASTRO. Verifiable Capabilities. [Team Names / GitHub Link]"

**Voiceover:**
*"LASTRO is building the trust layer for the future of education and work. Thank you."*
